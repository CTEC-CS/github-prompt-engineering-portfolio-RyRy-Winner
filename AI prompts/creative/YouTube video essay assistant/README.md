# Youtube video essay assistant

> *This prompt is intended to assist YouTube creators who are experiencing creative roadblocks by generating structured, targeted talking points for video essays.*

## Overview

The Youtube Video Essay Assistant prompt produces a focused list of video content ideas and brainstorming structures. By taking into account the user's explicit channel identity, niche, intended topic, and core viewpoint, it forces the AI to output tailored concepts that match the creator's creative voice.

This prompt solves the problems of creative block, structure loss, and information overload during the pre-production script phase. Instead of presenting loose suggestions or unnecessary conversational introductions, it narrows down the AI's response to clear talking points accompanied by highly regulated length constraints.

It is particularly useful for video essayists who have hit a dead end, creators wanting to preview a potential video's conceptual depth before recording, or YouTubers seeking fresh resource angles. It operates effectively by repeatedly steering the AI's attention back to the creator's stated background, forcing strict compliance with their analytical constraints.

**Best For:**
- YouTubers who have hit a creative roadblock and need new talking points
- Creators wanting to see what a potential video framework looks like before starting
- Video essayists looking for alternative source directions and starting places
- Ensuring script brainstorming stays entirely consistent with a channel's existing vibe

**Output:** An unadorned list of brainstorming talking points, where each generated point features a strict length constraint of 3-5 sentences per item.

## Quick Start

### 1. Copy the prompt
Open `prompt.md` and copy the entire template.

### 2. Fill in placeholders
Replace these with your specific information:
- `[channel type (animation, talk show, etc.)]` - The genre, presentation style, or category of your YouTube presence
- `[channel]` - Your specific YouTube channel name or handle to guide historical relevance
- `[topic]` - The primary thesis, concept, or event your video essay is covering
- `[viewpoint]` - The explicit perspective, stance, or lens you want the video to approach the topic from
- `[talking point]` - A concept or idea you have already developed to set a structural example

### 3. Run in your LLM
Paste into Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT, or your preferred model.

### 4. Review and adapt
Review the uncapped list of talking points generated by the AI. Filter through the concepts to pick your favorite elements, and expand upon the 3-5 sentence foundations to build your final video script.

## What Makes This Prompt Effective

**Repetitive Attention Steering:** The prompt forces the AI to lock onto your perspective by continuously reminding it of your specific channel handle, genre, and viewpoint variables throughout the input text.

**Real-World Exemplars:** Requiring you to include an existing talking point gives the model a clear blueprint of the exact analytical depth and creative tone required for subsequent suggestions.

**Strict Output Exclusion:** Command boundaries like "Only tell me the points" and "Don't include anything other than this" prevent the AI from generating conversational preambles or wrap-ups.

**Uncapped Volume Flexibility:** Instructing the model to bring up "as many points as possible" shifts the burden of curation to the human user, maximizing the raw creative variety outputted in a single run.

## Customization Tips

**For Broad Structural Planning:**
If you want to map out an entire season or series rather than a single video, change the topic variable to a macro-theme and request distinct video arcs instead of singular paragraph points.

**For Alternative Channel Frameworks:**
If your channel does not produce essays, swap out the channel type variable for your exact medium (e.g., vlogs, tech reviews) to align the suggestions with alternate editing pacing.

**Adjusting Detail Level:**
- **How to make output more detailed:** Modify the sentence length constraint from "3-5 sentences" to a higher range, or ask the AI to include secondary counter-arguments within each concept description.
- **How to make output more concise:** Specify a numeric ceiling to overwrite the uncapped volume clause if you only require a small handful of ideas.

## Limitations & Considerations

**What This Prompt Does Well:**
- Adapts fluidly to highly diverse channel niches and presentation formats.
- Maintains strict formatting boundaries without leaking conversational AI filler.
- Connects disparate cultural or historical concepts to a singular user viewpoint.

**What Requires Human Judgment:**
- Culling and organizing the raw volume of suggestions, as the prompt purposefully tells the AI to dump as many ideas as possible.
- Verifying historical or technical facts within the generated points, as the priority of the prompt is conceptual brainstorming rather than direct research verification.

**Always:**
- Review the suggested talking points to ensure they fit within your legal, ethical, and narrative boundaries before implementing them in a public video.
- Manually check real-world references brought up in the 3-5 sentence explanations to guarantee accuracy.
