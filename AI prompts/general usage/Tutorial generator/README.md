# Tutorial generator

> *This prompt serves as a basic guide generator for creating step-by-step tutorials whenever anyone needs to know how to perform a task or learn a skill.*

## Overview

The Tutorial Generator prompt produces an exhaustive, deeply informative, and fluff-free guide designed to teach complex tasks. By requiring a structured layout paired with creative elements, it transforms abstract instructions into highly memorable learning modules.

This prompt solves the issue of confusing, poorly structured educational guides and standard AI conversational filler. Instead of sifting through introductory pleasantries or superficial summaries, users receive high-density instructions that immediately zero in on the precise obstacles they are facing.

It is highly useful for students, hobbyists, gamers, or general learners who want to master a specific physical or digital capability without the typical AI "yapping." The prompt works effectively because it pairs strict content minimization constraints with commands that force high data depth, keeping learners thoroughly engaged and absorbed in the content.

**Best For:**
- Learning physical sports or techniques (e.g., riding a bike with no hands)
- Outdoor and stamina preparation (e.g., pacing techniques for hiking)
- Video game mechanics and technical skills (e.g., speed bridging in Minecraft Java edition)
- Comprehensive, uninterrupted study of a new hobby

**Output:** An exhaustive, deeply detailed numbered list where each instruction step features a custom, highly memorable title, entirely stripped of conversational AI fluff.

## Quick Start

### 1. Copy the prompt
Open `prompt.md` and copy the entire template.

### 2. Fill in placeholders
Replace these with your specific information:
- `[skill]` - The overarching topic or capability you are trying to learn
- `[specific part of skill (Or just say "most of it" for general help)]` - The exact bottleneck you are struggling with or a request for general guidance

### 3. Run in your LLM
Paste into Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT, or your preferred model.

### 4. Review and adapt
Review the sequential steps for physical safety or technical logic depending on the activity type. While the prompt explicitly blocks conversational fluff, verify that any small automated intro or conclusion remarks do not distract from the core instructional steps.

## What Makes This Prompt Effective

**Numbered Sequence Layout:** Demanding a structured, numbered execution plan ensures that returning to reference specific milestones during active practice is simple and frictionless.

**Memorable Titles per Item:** Requiring unique titles for every step anchors the concepts psychologically, making complex micro-skills easier for the human brain to retain over time.

**Anti-Fluff Constraint:** Explicitly ordering the elimination of standard AI introductory/concluding filler forces the model to maximize actionable data density.

**Uncapped Information Volume:** Telling the AI to "add as many things as you need" allows the system to exhaust its full repository of knowledge on the topic, ensuring a comprehensive breakdown.

## Customization Tips

**For General Knowledge Exploration:**
If you do not have a specific hurdle, input "most of it" into the secondary placeholder to generate a broad, high-density foundational index on the topic.

**For Highly Complex or Technical Skills:**
Isolate specific sub-actions in the second placeholder to prevent the AI from generalized overviews, forcing it to apply the step-by-step treatment to micro-mechanics.

**Adjusting Detail Level:**
- **How to make output more detailed:** The prompt naturally trends toward maximum depth due to the uncapped volume clause, but you can explicitly request deeper technical specifications per step if needed.
- **How to make output more concise:** Specify a hard cap on the number of sequence steps if you require a brief, high-level overview instead of an exhaustive deep-dive.

## Limitations & Considerations

**What This Prompt Does Well:**
- Completely minimizes standard conversational fluff and repetitive phrasing.
- Discovers practical, non-obvious tips for a vast array of hobbies and niches.
- Automatically generates high-volume, highly engaging, and readable copy.

**What Requires Human Judgment:**
- Verifying the safety of physical instructions (such as riding a bicycle with no hands) before attempting them in real life.
- Accounting for occasional minor intro or outro sentences that some base LLMs default to despite anti-fluff rules.

**Always:**
- Apply common sense to step-by-step physical routines generated by the AI to ensure personal safety.
- Cross-reference highly technical software or gaming steps to confirm they align with current patch versions or mechanical realities.
