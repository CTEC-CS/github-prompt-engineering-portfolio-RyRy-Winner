# Influencer/Content creator Ad Script Creator

> *This prompt allows small business owners and creators to generate high-energy advertising scripts tailored for TikTok or Reels to promote products without any filler.*

## Overview

The Influencer/Content creator Ad Script Creator prompt produces a highly optimized, two-part marketing asset tailored specifically to a creator's niche and a company's product line. By establishing a rigid dividing line across the response structure, it forces the AI to output creative script narrative alongside raw baseline talking points.

This prompt solves the issue of repetitive marketing angles, unorganized pitch points, and standard AI conversational preamble ("blubber") that creators typically have to edit out. Instead of receiving a single block of text or basic bullet points, the user gets a production-ready promotional layout divided cleanly by functional utility.

It is uniquely beneficial for small business owners looking to maximize social engagement, social media influencers pitching across different categories, or digital content creators trying to pitch products seamlessly to their target demographics. The prompt remains highly effective because it explicitly captures individual channel details and product properties, allowing the model to generate a targeted script that feels highly personalized.

**Best For:**
- Small business owners generating high-energy scripts for TikTok or Reels
- Animation YouTubers seeking structured promotional segments
- Gaming or specialized content creators integrating technical sponsorships
- Influencers looking to push a brand nonchalantly or natively inside a video

**Output:** A dual-structured response consisting of a script of designated duration in the first half and a key talking points bulleted list in the second half, strictly stripped of all intro/outro conversational text.

## Quick Start

### 1. Copy the prompt
Open `prompt.md` and copy the entire template.

### 2. Fill in placeholders
Replace these with your specific information:
- `[company]` - The brand or business sponsoring the video content
- `[product]` - The specific item, website, or service being advertised
- `[video topic]` - The overarching theme or subject matter of your main video
- `[channel type (AKA Animation, talk show, gaming, etc)]` - Your channel's primary content genre or style
- `[duration]` - The requested runtime length for the generated advertisement script

### 3. Run in your LLM
Paste into Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT, or your preferred model.

### 4. Review and adapt
Review the script provided in the first half for natural flow. Use the isolated bullet points in the second half to make rapid adjustments, ensuring the dialogue sounds organic to your personal speaking style while still hitting every necessary brand requirement.

## What Makes This Prompt Effective

**Split-Response Segments:** Forcing the AI to divide its answer into two specific halves allows the creator to look at real script copy while maintaining a high-level view of core thematic requirements.

**Niche & Product Specificity:** Pairing channel type with product parameters ensures that the AI contextualizes the pitch, keeping an animation script distinct from a gaming script.

**Anti-Blubber Mandate:** Demanding that the model include "nothing but these 2 topics" strips out standard chatbot commentary, reducing preparation and editing friction.

**Isolated Talking Points:** Extracting the main points separately allows the writer to easily edit or rewrite the final draft to better match their real-life personality while ensuring no contractual criteria are missed.

## Customization Tips

**For Specialized or Interactive Media:**
If you require specialized call-to-actions (such as QR codes or link-in-bio highlights), add them into the product placeholder alongside the name to force them directly into the script actions.

**For Highly Interactive Content Formats:**
If your video topic relies on a strong comedic angle, use the channel type variable to specify a performance constraint (e.g., "fast-paced sketch comedy channel").

**Adjusting Detail Level:**
- **How to make output more detailed:** Add specific brand requirements or required taglines directly inside the product variables.
- **How to make output more concise:** Tighten the duration parameter down to precise micro-ad ranges (e.g., "a 15-second hook script").

## Limitations & Considerations

**What This Prompt Does Well:**
- Standardizes output into two perfectly separated, easy-to-read operational categories.
- Strips out extra AI conversational filler flawlessly across multiple runs.
- Adapts tone rapidly between fast-paced gaming environments and nonchalant influencer vibes.

**What Requires Human Judgment:**
- Timing the script reads manually to ensure the AI's pacing matches your real-world talking speed for the requested duration.
- Fine-tuning the vocabulary choices, as base LLMs can sometimes drift back into cliché marketing copy if the product description is left too broad.

**Always:**
- Verify that the final response contains exactly the two requested sections without conversational filler.
- Read the generated draft aloud to check that the transitions sound natural for your voice.
